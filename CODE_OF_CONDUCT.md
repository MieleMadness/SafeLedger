# SafeLedger Collaboration Standards

SafeLedger welcomes constructive technical participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Contributors are expected to keep project discussions professional, specific, and focused on the work. Technical disagreement is welcome when it is supported by evidence and presented respectfully.

Protect privacy. Do not post real customer data, recovery material, credentials, signing secrets, or other private information in issues, pull requests, reviews, examples, or test fixtures.

Security findings that could put users at risk should follow `SECURITY.md` rather than being published with unnecessary sensitive detail.

Project maintainers may moderate repository discussions or contributions that are disruptive, expose private information, or make productive collaboration difficult. Repeated problems may result in participation restrictions.

These standards apply to SafeLedger repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed collaboration spaces.
