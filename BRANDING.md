# SafeLedger branding

The official product name is **SafeLedger**. The owner-approved mark is the blue and black shield divided by a white, sweeping S. Use this mark unchanged for product, social and agent identity. Do not substitute a generic padlock, shield or alternate wordmark.

## Brand colors

| Color | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| SafeLedger blue | `#0099FF` |
| Black | `#000000` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |

Blue is the primary brand accent. Maintain the white S and adequate contrast on light or dark backgrounds. Seasonal graphics may use supporting colors without recoloring the mark. Preserve proportions and clear space.

## Repository assets

- [`sl.png`](sl.png): existing transparent shield for general use.
- [`build/icon-source.png`](build/icon-source.png): existing matching packaging icon; retain transparency and the established application icon.
- [`build/icon.svg`](build/icon.svg): vector trace aligned with the owner-approved shield; use for scalable layouts.

Use the original raster mark for avatars and social graphics when available. The vector is a faithful trace, not the original source artwork. Do not stretch, crop the shield, or add effects to the mark itself.

Brand voice: calm, technically accurate, accessible, privacy-first. SafeLedger is a free, local-first, offline encrypted information vault, not a cryptocurrency wallet or custodian. Avoid absolute security guarantees.
